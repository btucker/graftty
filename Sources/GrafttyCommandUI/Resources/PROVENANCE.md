# Provider marks

These marks identify the provider of an agent session. They are not Graftty branding.

- `claude.png`: Anthropic's monochrome template mark, copied from the Claude desktop app's `Contents/Resources/TrayIconTemplate@3x.png` on 2026-10-31. Original size: 72 × 72.
- `codex.svg`: OpenAI's monochrome Codex mark, from the ChatGPT desktop app's `Contents/Resources/app.asar`, entry `webview/assets/codex-d905da579253.svg`, on 2026-10-31. The view box is 24 × 24.
- `codex.png`: transparent 72 × 72 rendering of that SVG for SwiftUI on Mac and iOS.

The native views render both PNGs as template images with semantic foreground tint. The original shapes are unchanged. Provider names remain accessible to screen readers. The provider marks remain the property of their respective owners.
