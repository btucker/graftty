Phone: zoom out to the gallery

A phone can't show the stage and the gallery at once, so it shows one or the other. The stage is today's terminal screen. Zooming out replaces it with the gallery, where every agent is a small terminal with its rail on the right, the same rule as the desktop.

Pinch to zoom the gallery

The gallery has two sizes, and the tiles always fill the screen. The stage is a separate view, not a third size.

  • Two views only: stage and gallery. You type on the stage. The gallery is for watching, scrolling and scrubbing.
  • Pinch moves between 4-up and 2-up, and goes no further. The tile under your fingers is one of the two that stay.
  • Tap a tile to put it on stage. Tap the agent's name on the stage to return to the gallery.
  • The last tile is always an empty one for starting a new agent. With three agents it fills the fourth slot; with four it is the first thing you reach by scrolling.
  • More agents than slots: scroll. With six agents, 4-up scrolls down to the other two.
  • 2-up is the phone's side by side. Each half is about 390 by 380 points, a real terminal you can read and scroll.
  • The gallery remembers the size you left it at.

Calls I made

  • 4-up is the default gallery size. A tile is about 170 by 220 points, so its rail is three times the height of a desktop tile's.
  • Tiles keep a fixed position, in the order agents were started. The one that needs you is marked, not moved.
  • The caption's second line carries the meaning: the question for an asking agent, the result for a finished one, the status otherwise.
  • Opening a worktree still lands on the agent that most needs you, as today, not on the gallery.
  • Pinch on the terminal stays text size, as today. The gallery is reached by tapping the agent's name.
  • Shells are left out of this sketch. Today's menu also offers Terminal and About this worktree; those need a home.
