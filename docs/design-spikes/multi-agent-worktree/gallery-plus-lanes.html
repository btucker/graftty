Calls that apply to both

These are my proposals, open to correction.

  • The lanes belong to whoever is on stage. The time axis is that agent's scrollback. Swap someone else onto the stage and the lanes redraw around them.
  • Two on stage means two rails, as today, each drawn around its own agent.
  • Clicking a tile or a lane swaps that agent onto the stage. Dragging it onto the stage puts it side by side.
  • Finished agents leave the gallery but stay in the lanes as history, with their result one hover away.
