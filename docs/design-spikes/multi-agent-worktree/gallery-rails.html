How it behaves

  • One rule everywhere: a terminal, then its rail on the right, with time running downward. The stage and each tile follow it.
  • Hovering a tile's rail magnifies the nearby marks and stacks the preview cards to its left, just as the main rail does. The cards cover the tile and spill over the terminal as far as they need.
  • Clicking a mark brings that agent on stage, scrolled to that message.
  • Clicking the tile body swaps that agent onto the stage; dragging it onto the stage puts it side by side.
  • The last tile is an empty one. Clicking it starts a new agent in this worktree, which then takes that place.
  • Swapping is a change of size, not of kind: the tile and its rail grow into the stage and its rail.

Known limit: a tile rail is about 70 points tall, so a long history crowds. Magnification on hover is what keeps it usable; taller tiles when there are only one or two helpers would help too.
