My pick is A.

The existing brief still answers “where are we?” and the timeline answers “how did we get here?” Removing the disclosure and adding time makes that relationship clearer.

The question behind “always available”

Does history need to be visible while you read the brief, or simply never collapsed? B guarantees both stay on screen. A uses the space more generously.
