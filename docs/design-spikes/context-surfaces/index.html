Theme behavior

These studies blend the Ghostty background toward its foreground. Dark palettes use more separation than light ones. No fixed gray, blue panel fill, or reliance on blur. A production implementation would also check low-contrast and unusual custom themes.

The layout remains distinct through the edge, inset, or section break even when a palette offers limited color separation.

Sample content and palettes. References: macOS design skill for native hierarchy, type and restrained chrome; design-taste-frontend for audit-first exploration and explicit tradeoffs. Theme requirements and top alignment take precedence over generic styling defaults.
